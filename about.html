About Extrabotics

Founded with a passion for innovation, Extrabotics is at the forefront of robotics technology, education, and competitive excellence.

About Us

At Extrabotics, we live and breathe robotics. We talk to robots, we command non-living components,
and they respond to our orders with precision. Robotics isn’t just technology for us — it’s an art form, a movement, and a community.
We founded Extrabotics with one mission:to make robotics popular and accessible by engaging robots in extraordinary tasks. Through creativity, competition, and education, we inspire the next generation of innovators.
What We Do
We train students, enthusiasts, and professionals to design, build, and deploy robots capable of tackling exciting challenges such as:
1. SoccerBots – robots that play football with strategy and teamwork.
2. Battle Bots – robots designed for head-to-head combat and endurance.
3. Mission Bots – themed robots solving real-world issues like road safety.
4. Maze Solving Bots – robots that navigate complex mazes with logic.
5. Racing Bots – high-speed robots built for track competitions.
6. Sumo Bots – robots that push, shove, and outwit their opponents in the ring.
Our Impact
* Hosting robotics competitions across multiple categories.
* Awarding competitors for innovation, teamwork, and excellence.
* Creating exciting events that educate, inspire, and entertain spectators.
* Building a strong robotics community passionate about solving problems and pushing boundaries.
👉 Join us as we continue to train, innovate, and showcase robotics in ways the world has never seen before.
