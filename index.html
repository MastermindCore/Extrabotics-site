Who We Are

Driven by a Mission to Inspire

"Making cutting-edge robotics accessible through education, competition, and community."

At Extrabotics, we believe in the transformative power of robotics to solve real-world problems and inspire the next generation. We operate at the intersection of technology and education.

From competitive events to educational workshops, everything we do is anchored in one goal: empowering Kenya's youth to lead the future of technology.
